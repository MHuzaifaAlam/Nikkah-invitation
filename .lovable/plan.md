# Cinematic Nikkah Invitation

## Goal
Create a mobile-first, fully scrollable invitation for Huzaifa and Bismah. Guests first open a full-screen luxury blush envelope, then move through a continuous palace-inspired story from welcome to final thanks.

## Opening Experience
- Fill the first viewport with layered blush, dusty-rose, and champagne-pink envelope folds.
- Add subtle paper grain, darker edges, restrained rose-gold borders, Islamic linework, and floral corner ornaments.
- Center a physically pressed deep-rose wax seal with metallic edging and embossed “H & B”.
- Open on click or keyboard input: lift the flap, release the folds, reveal the ivory palace courtyard, and guide the guest into the invitation.

## Scrolling Story
Build the requested sequence as connected, full-width chapters:
1. Palace welcome with Bismillah, Qur’anic quote, names, and invitation line.
2. Bright “With Love & Gratitude” message with drifting petals.
3. “The Couple” with ornate, non-photographic groom and bride placeholders.
4. “Our Journey” vertical timeline with three milestones and a line that reveals on scroll.
5. “The Nikkah” with configurable date, time, venue, and address cards.
6. Live countdown prepared for a future confirmed date.
7. Cinematic quote chapter with the three supplied blessings.
8. Calm Islamic dua chapter.
9. “Join Us” venue chapter and location-ready action.
10. RSVP chapter with configurable link.
11. Final love message, final dua, thank-you screen, and minimal footer.

## Visual Direction
- Use blush pink, rose gold, champagne, pearl, ivory, and restrained soft green throughout.
- Keep the palace ivory with warm light, flowers, fountain reflections, and a blush evening atmosphere.
- Repeat arches, fine geometric patterns, gold rules, and botanical ornaments so sections feel like pages of one invitation.
- Use elegant serif/calligraphic display typography with a clean, light supporting typeface.
- Keep gold details sparse and precise; avoid dark, neon, oversaturated, or childish styling.

## Motion and Controls
- Add subtle viewport reveals, image scaling, staggered cards, timeline drawing, floating petals, and slow background movement.
- Add a thin rose-gold scroll progress line.
- Show a bottom-right music toggle only after the envelope opens; audio begins only after user interaction and remains optional.
- Add a back-to-top control near the ending.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Content and Configuration
- Keep all unknown event details as “To be announced”.
- Centralize wedding details and RSVP URL in one configuration object.
- Do not invent photographs or fake people; use decorative portrait frames until real images are supplied.
- Keep location and RSVP actions in a safe placeholder state until real destinations are provided.

## Quality Checks
- Verify closed envelope, opening transition, and full scrolling state on desktop and mobile.
- Check text fit, countdown layout, timeline stacking, background crops, touch targets, keyboard access, overflow, and contrast.
- Add unique invitation metadata to the home page and confirm the latest preview build has no errors.
