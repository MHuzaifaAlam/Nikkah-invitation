# Rose Gold Nikkah

IMPORTANT — ENVELOPE COLOR MUST BE PINK

Change the entire full-screen envelope design to a luxurious romantic pink palette.

The envelope should NOT be ivory/cream as the dominant color.

The dominant envelope color should be:

Soft Blush / Rose Pink

Use a sophisticated combination of:

Dusty rose

Blush pink

Soft pastel pink

Champagne pink

Rose gold

Pearl pink

Very subtle cream highlights

Avoid:

Neon pink

Hot pink

Barbie pink

Oversaturated pink

Cheap-looking gradients

The goal is luxury romantic pink, suitable for an elegant Nikkah invitation.

FULL-SCREEN PINK ENVELOPE

The first screen should be completely covered by the pink envelope.

Think of a luxurious handcrafted wedding envelope made from:

soft blush-pink velvet/paper with rose-gold detailing.

Use subtle texture so it doesn't look like a flat pink website.

The envelope should have:

Blush pink base

Slightly darker pink folds

Rose-gold borders

Gold embossed Islamic patterns

Floral ornaments

Subtle paper/velvet texture

Soft highlights

Elegant shadows

Slightly darker pink edges

Champagne/gold decorative details

RECOMMENDED COLOR PALETTE

Use CSS variables approximately like:

:root {
  --blush-pink: #F3C6CF;
  --soft-pink: #EFB6C1;
  --dusty-rose: #D98F9E;
  --rose-pink: #C97889;
  --deep-rose: #9E4F62;

  --rose-gold: #C98F87;
  --champagne-gold: #D8B27C;
  --soft-gold: #E5C58E;

  --pearl: #FFF8F5;
  --warm-white: #FFFDFB;

  --text: #59343C;
}


The exact values can be adjusted to achieve the most premium appearance.

PINK ENVELOPE + PALACE

The palace/fountain environment should complement the pink envelope.

Use a romantic atmosphere with:

Soft pink evening sky

Warm palace lighting

Rose-gold architectural highlights

Blush flowers

White flowers

Marble fountain

Soft golden lanterns

Gentle mist

Warm reflections in the water

The palace should NOT be completely pink.

Instead:

Pink envelope + elegant ivory palace + warm gold lighting + subtle blush atmosphere.

This prevents the whole screen from becoming overwhelmingly pink.

CENTER WAX SEAL

The center wax seal should complement the pink envelope.

Use:

Rose-gold / deep dusty-rose wax seal

Inside:

H & B

The seal can have:

Rose-gold metallic edge

Deep rose center

Gold embossed H & B

Subtle glossy wax texture

Soft glow

The seal should look physically pressed into the pink envelope.

CENTER TEXT

Under the seal:

Huzaifa & Bismah

Then:

Tap to Open

Typography should be:

Elegant

Romantic

Thin

Sophisticated

Use champagne/gold or deep rose text depending on contrast.

ENVELOPE FOLDS

The envelope should have visible physical folds.

Use slightly different shades of pink:

Main surface → soft blush

Side folds → dusty pink

Bottom fold → slightly darker rose

Top flap → soft champagne-blush gradient

This will make the envelope look three-dimensional rather than like a flat pink background.

GOLD / ROSE-GOLD DETAILS

Use gold carefully.

Do not cover everything in gold.

Use gold/rose-gold for:

Border

Wax seal

Islamic ornaments

H & B

Small decorative lines

Floral details

Light reflections

The overall balance should be approximately:

70% pink
20% soft/pearl tones
10% gold/rose-gold

OVERALL EMOTIONAL FEELING

The opening screen should feel like:

A luxurious blush-pink wedding envelope sitting in front of a beautiful royal palace courtyard.

It should communicate:

💕 Romance
🌸 Softness
✨ Luxury
🏛️ Royal elegance
🌙 Islamic/Nikkah atmosphere
💍 Marriage
🌹 Celebration

It should NOT feel childish or overly feminine.

The design must remain sophisticated enough for both Huzaifa and Bismah.

IMPORTANT

Do NOT use the previous dark brown/black design.

Do NOT use a plain pink background.

Do NOT create a small pink envelope floating in the middle.

The requirement is:

FULL-SCREEN PINK LUXURY ENVELOPE + CENTER WAX SEAL + PALACE/FOUNTAIN ATMOSPHERE + CINEMATIC OPENING ANIMATION.
